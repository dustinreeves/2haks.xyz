👑 How to play

  1. Build a level. Pick a tile below the game and click or tap to place it. Right-click erases.
  2. Start the wave. A horde of heroes runs from the 🚪 door to the 🚩 flag.
  3. You are the boss! Move with ← → (or A D), jump with ↑ / W, attack with Space. Heroes who land on your head hurt you! After squishing a hero, your boss needs a moment to recover (it looks see-through), so use attacks and traps too.
  4. Every hero you stop gives you 5 🪙 golden coins. Spend them in the shop on new traps, new bosses and upgrades.
  5. Stop more than half the heroes to win the wave. Each wave brings more, and smarter, heroes.

Levels must be fair: heroes need a possible way to reach the flag. Use 👣 Show route to see it.
