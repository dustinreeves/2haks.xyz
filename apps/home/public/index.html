How it works

  1. Come up with an idea. Usually a dumb one. “What if you played the boss?”
  2. Build it with an AI. We describe it, Claude writes most of the code, and we argue about the details.
  3. Play it, break it, fix it. Then it goes live on its own something.2haks.xyz address.
